About

More than a developer. A builder of experiences.

Computer science graduate from KFUEIT, working as a Flutter developer on production apps and games.

I specialise in cross-platform mobile apps with Flutter, Dart, Firebase and REST APIs. At VirtueNetz I’ve taken client and company products all the way to the Google Play Store and the App Store.

Alongside apps, I work on 2D games in Unity and C#: movement, levels, enemies, cutscenes and Android release. I’m also the founder of Meteredo and a co-founder of Hairalize, our award-winning final year project.

I care about clean structure, interfaces people can use without thinking, and software that solves an everyday problem.
